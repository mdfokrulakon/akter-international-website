About the company

A Dhaka-based company built around two disciplines: keeping institutions supplied, and keeping businesses online.

Akter International was established in 2023 and operates across distribution, institutional supply, and technology services from its office in Khilgaon, Dhaka.

Our distribution business covers consumer products, stationery, office supplies, hospital and medical supplies, school supplies, corporate supplies, computer accessories, electronics, toiletries, and general institutional supplies.

We are a distributor of En-Lac, supplied through HS Corporation, and a distributor of Good Luck Stationery in the Khilgaon area. Akter International is an enlisted vendor of the Islamic Bank Foundation and supplies products to Debidwar Evergreen Hospital. We have also previously supplied stationery items to selected branches of IFIC Bank, BRAC Bank, and Mutual Trust Bank (MTB).

More recently, Akter International has expanded into technology — website development, custom web application development, maintenance, SEO, and digital marketing — to help clients build their digital presence alongside their physical supply chain.
